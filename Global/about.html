About Global Logistics

Our Story

Founded in 2005, Global Logistics started as a small freight forwarding company in Chicago. Over the past two decades, we have grown into a worldwide logistics powerhouse, serving over 10,000 businesses across 150+ countries.

Our mission is simple: to make global trade accessible, reliable, and transparent for businesses of all sizes. We believe that logistics should never be a bottleneck to growth.

Our Mission

To provide seamless, technology-driven logistics solutions that empower businesses to reach new markets and customers without the traditional complexities of international shipping.
