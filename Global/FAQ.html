Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding tracking, customer accounts, and logistics services.

How do I track my parcel?
Log into your user account on the tracking page, enter your unique tracking ID provided in your receipt, and hit "Track" to see live updates.
Why do I need to register before tracking?
Account registration ensures package data privacy and allows us to notify you directly via email when package statuses change.
What should I do if my package is delayed?
Check live status logs for potential customs holds. If delayed beyond expected dates, submit a claim on our Refund Policy page.
How can I contact customer support?
Use the floating support chat widget at the bottom right of any page or send us a message through our Contact page.
