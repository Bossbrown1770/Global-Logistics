Refund & Guarantee Policy

Transparent rules regarding delivery guarantees, losses, and damages.

1. Eligibility for Refund

Clients are eligible for partial or full refunds under the following conditions:

  • Service Delays: Expedited shipments missing guaranteed delivery windows by more than 48 hours.
  • Damaged Goods: Physical damage incurred during transport within our handling network.
  • Lost Packages: Shipments unlocated after 14 days from projected arrival date.

2. Refund Process & Timeline

Submitted claims are evaluated within 3-5 business days. Approved refunds are returned via original payment methods within 7-10 business days.
